Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about requesting a free digital design proof & pricing.

How fast will I receive my digital design proof? ➕
Our Florida-based design team typically sends your photorealistic 3D proof and exact price quote within 2 to 12 hours directly to your email.
Is receiving a digital design proof really 100% free? ➕
Yes, 100% free with zero obligation! We will design your neon layout, calculate pricing, and send you the digital mockup completely free of charge.
What if I only have a low-res photo, logo, or rough hand sketch? ➕
No problem at all! You can upload hand drawings, screenshots, low-resolution logos, or vector files. Our graphic designers will vectorize and optimize your artwork into clean neon tubing paths.
Can I request revisions or changes after seeing the proof? ➕
Absolutely! You can request as many adjustments to colors, dimensions, and styling as you want until you are 100% in love with the layout before ordering.
How do I hang and power my neon sign? ➕
Every custom sign includes pre-drilled holes in the acrylic backing and stainless steel standoff mounting screws. Simply mount it like a picture frame, plug the included power adapter into any standard wall outlet, and enjoy!
